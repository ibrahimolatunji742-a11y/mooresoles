01

Brand Strategy

MOORE is built on one idea: what carries a person and what surrounds them should be made with the same seriousness.

Mission

To hand-make footwear and gold that hold up under real scrutiny — the kind a person gives an object they intend to keep for twenty years — and to build that craft on African soil for a global clientele.

Vision

To become the house international buyers name in the same breath as the footwear and fine-jewelry maisons of Europe, while remaining unmistakably rooted in West African material culture, craft lineage, and gold tradition.

Values

  • Craft over volume. Small runs, named artisans, visible skill.
  • Material honesty. Full-grain leather, solid gold and gold vermeil stated plainly — no plating passed off as more than it is.
  • Earned exclusivity. Scarcity comes from production capacity, not artificial hype.
  • Rootedness without costume. The African identity of the house shows in material sourcing, technique, and story — not in literal motifs or "tribal" decoration.
  • Quiet confidence. The product should not need to shout to be recognized.

Positioning

MOORE sits in the accessible-luxury tier at launch — priced above premium mass footwear and gold-plated fashion jewelry, below the established European maisons — with a five-to-ten-year path toward full maison status as heritage, waiting lists, and resale value accumulate.

Unique selling proposition

MOORE is one of very few houses that designs footwear and fine gold jewelry under a single point of view, on the premise that a person's presence is built from the ground up: what carries them, and what surrounds them. Every piece is handmade in limited runs by named artisans, with the maker's mark inside.

Target customer

Self-made professionals and entrepreneurs, roughly 28–55, in Lagos, Accra, Nairobi, London, Dubai, Houston and Atlanta's diaspora hubs. First-generation or newly consolidated wealth. Buys fewer, better things. Values provenance and story over logo visibility.

Brand personality

Composed, warm, precise, self-assured, unhurried. Speaks like someone who has already arrived — never like someone trying to prove it.

What sets MOORE apart

  • Footwear and gold under one house, not two separate lines bolted together — the two categories share a single design philosophy and are marketed as one presence, not two products.
  • An African-rooted supply chain and craft base presented at global luxury standard, rather than positioned as a "heritage" or "ethical" niche brand.
  • Named-artisan production: every piece can be traced to the hands that made it, which most mass luxury brands cannot offer at this price point.
  • A founder's-name house (MOORE) rather than an invented lifestyle word — closer to how Ferragamo, Loubain, or Cartier are built than how most new footwear labels are named.
02

Brand Name

MOORE SOLES AND AURA reads as a house name, not a product name — the difference between a label and a lineage.

What the name communicates

MOORE functions as a surname would for a fashion or jewelry house — it gives the brand a founder, an accountable name, and a dynastic quality that can carry into a second generation. It is also short, phonetically confident, and reads cleanly on packaging, hardware, and gold engraving at small sizes.

SOLES is the literal anchor to footwear, but it also carries the sense of foundation and departure point — the part of a person that touches the ground first, and the part that starts every journey.

AURA is the anchor to gold and jewelry — presence, light, the effect a person has on a room before they speak. Gold is the material that has always been used to signal exactly this, across West African goldsmithing traditions in particular.

Together, the full name proposes a philosophy: soles carry you toward where you are going; aura is how you arrive. Footwear and gold are framed as two ends of the same idea of presence, not two unrelated product lines.

Five alternative variations (MOORE retained as the core identity)

  1. 01
    MOORE HOUSE — reads immediately as a fashion/jewelry maison; simplest to license and print small.
  2. 02
    MOORE & AURA — shortens the descriptive identity while keeping the core walking/gold duality implied.
  3. 03
    MOORE ATELIER — leans hardest into handcraft and custom-order positioning.
  4. 04
    THE MOORE LEGACY — useful for a heritage or archive sub-line, or a founder's-collection tier.
  5. 05
    MOORE GOLD & LEATHER — the most literal and functional variant, strong for a wholesale or B2B-facing name if the consumer-facing name ever needs a plain-language counterpart.
03

Logo Concepts

Five directions, each built to work at three very different scales: embossed into leather, engraved into gold, and rendered on a phone screen.

1 · The Signature Arch

Symbol: a single continuous line forms an M whose middle stroke curves like the arch of a shoe sole. Type: none required — the mark can stand alone. Proportions: wide, low-height mark (roughly 2:1) so it sits comfortably along a shoe's inner heel or a bracelet's inner face. Placement: leather insoles, gold bracelet interiors, embossed foil on boxes.

2 · MSA Monogram Seal

Symbol: circular seal with M and A interlocked at the centre; S rendered as a fine horizontal line beneath them, reading as a footbridge or a horizon line. Type: none inside the seal; house name in small caps may ring the outer edge for stamps and certificates. Proportions: true circle, works from 8mm (ring stamp) to poster scale. Placement: wax seals on boxes, certificate cards, cufflink-scale gold stamping.

3 · The MOORE Wordmark

Type: a custom-spaced serif, close in spirit to Playfair or Canela, tracked out slightly wider than default. Symbol: the second O is replaced with a small solid gold disc, functioning as a pendant motif. Proportions: full wordmark, letterforms at even width. Placement: primary lockup for the website, storefront fascia, shopping bags.

4 · The Aura Ring

Symbol: a thin single circle (the "aura") surrounding a minimal, geometric M reduced to three straight strokes. Type: none. Proportions: near-square, extremely reductive — designed specifically to survive laser engraving on gold at ring and pendant scale. Placement: the house's jewelry-only mark, hallmark-style, stamped inside rings and on chain clasps.

5 · The M/S/A Cipher

Symbol: the three initials stacked vertically rather than side by side, each letter reduced to its simplest geometric stroke, forming a narrow totem-like mark. Type: none. Proportions: tall and narrow (roughly 1:3), designed for hardware — zipper pulls, box corners, hangtag edges — where horizontal space is limited. Placement: hangtags, dust-bag drawstring ends, hardware stamping on bags and boots.

Recommendation

Use Concept 3 (The MOORE Wordmark) as the primary public-facing lockup, Concept 4 (The Aura Ring) as the dedicated jewelry hallmark, and Concept 2 (the Monogram Seal) for packaging and certificates. This gives the house one wordmark and two symbolic marks — enough range for footwear, gold, and paper goods without the identity feeling scattered.

A palette built around restraint — gold is earned by scarcity, not by covering every surface.

Antique Gold
#C6A664

Warm Cream
#F3EEE4

#3B2A20
Leather-adjacent tone for print, boxes, seasonal accents.
Champagne
#E8DCC4
Jewelry-line backgrounds, dust bags, soft highlights.
Soft White
#FAF9F6
Body copy on dark, certificate cards, negative space.

Application rule

Obsidian and cream carry the brand in roughly a 60/30 split; gold is held to under 10% of any given surface — a hairline rule, a foil stamp, a single stitched thread — so that when it does appear, it still reads as precious rather than decorative.

Product Collections

Every collection is named to describe a moment, not just a shoe or a stone.

Footwear

The Boardroom Line

Men's dress shoes — oxfords and derbies in Cordovan and black calfskin, built for a man whose day starts in a meeting and doesn't end at one.

The Aura Heel

Women's heels — a sculpted, low-arch heel designed for a full evening of standing, not just an entrance.

The Sunday Loafer

Unstructured penny and horsebit loafers for the day after the deal closes.

The Harmattan Sandal

Open leather sandals referencing West African craft sandal-making, elevated in tanned full-grain leather.

The Founder's Boot

Chelsea and lace-up boots built for travel — the shoe of someone building something from scratch.

The Off-Duty

Minimalist leather sneakers, MOORE's one concession to casual wear, still hand-stitched.

Bespoke by MOORE — a fully custom footwear service: client measurements, choice of hide, and a hand-engraved sole plate with the owner's initials in the Aura Ring mark.

Gold

The Presence Chain

Solid gold curb and figaro chains, sized to be worn daily rather than saved for occasions.

The Signet Line

Signet and statement rings, several engravable with the Aura Ring hallmark or a client's own initial.

The Aura Cuff

Solid and hollow-core gold bangles and cuffs, designed to be worn stacked or alone.

The Golden Hour

Earrings — hoops, drops, and studs named for the light gold is meant to catch.

The Anchor Pendant

Pendants built around a single grounding motif, echoing the "soles" idea of foundation, in gold on fine chain.

Commissioned Gold — the jewelry counterpart to Bespoke by MOORE: one-of-one pieces designed with the client, cast and finished by named goldsmiths.

Brand Story

Before anyone hears you speak, they have already seen how you walked in.

MOORE was built on a simple observation: the two things people notice about a person entering a room — before a word is exchanged — are what's on their feet and what surrounds them. One carries you forward. The other announces that you've arrived.

We make both, by hand, because we don't believe they should be treated as separate crafts. A sole is stitched the way a chain is soldered — with the understanding that it will be trusted for years, not one season. Every pair of shoes that leaves our workshop has been shaped, cut, and finished by someone whose name we can tell you. Every gold piece has passed through a goldsmith's hands more times than it strictly needed to, because "good enough" was never the standard we were building toward.

We started this house rooted in West African leatherwork and goldsmithing — traditions that have always understood presence as something built from the ground up, not applied at the last minute. We are not interested in performing that heritage for an audience. We are interested in building it into something that holds its own on any street, in any city, next to any house with a longer history than ours.

MOORE is for the walk in, and for what people remember after you've left. Your soles carry you toward where you're going. Your aura is how you arrive.

Launch Campaign — "Walk With Aura"

Campaign story

A short film and photo series following three people — a lawyer, a musician, and a goldsmith's daughter turned architect — through the last thirty seconds before they walk into a room that matters to them: a courtroom, a stage, a client meeting. The film never shows their faces until they're through the door. Until then, the camera stays on their feet and their hands. The line that closes the film: "Everything that got you here is already on you."

Visual direction

Low, warm, late-afternoon light. Obsidian and cream as the dominant palette on set, with gold appearing only where it's actually worn — never as a set decoration. Architecture with strong horizontal lines (walkways, hallways, bridges) to echo the "soles" motif.

Photography style

Documentary-adjacent, shot mostly at eye level and foot level, minimal retouching, natural skin and hand texture kept visible — the craft argument only works if the imagery looks as honest as the product.

Models

Real clients and working professionals rather than professional models where possible; one or two professional models used for scale shots only.

Locations

Lagos (Victoria Island rooftop, Third Mainland Bridge walkway), a London chambers building, a New York gallery — three cities, one visual language.

Clothing

Tailored, minimal, dark-neutral wardrobe styled to keep the shoes and gold as the only warm-toned elements in frame.

Product styling

One hero product per subject — never full head-to-toe styling — so the shoe or the chain reads as the single deliberate choice.

Campaign captions

  • Everything that got you here is already on you.
  • Thirty seconds before the room finds out who you are.
  • Walk in. Let the room catch up.

Short TikTok/Reels concepts

  • 15-second cut: feet only, walking toward a door, cut to black before it opens.
  • Split screen: the shoe being made / the same shoe walking into the courtroom.
  • Behind-the-scenes: the three subjects reacting to the finished film for the first time.

Launch strategy

Seed the film to a small group of tastemakers and existing clients 48 hours before public release. Public launch paired with a single-location pop-up (Lagos) offering first access to the debut Boardroom Line and Presence Chain pieces, with a waitlist opened simultaneously for international shipping.
